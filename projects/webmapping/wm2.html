Key Locations in TU Wien Campus.

This page consists of two location maps. The first map is a hand-coded static HTML/CSS campus map of TU Wien, and the other one is an interactive Leaflet map of the same area. The maps show four significant buildings on TU Wien main campus at Resselgasse, Vienna's 4th district. These areas are: the Kuppelsaal (Domed Hall), the Bibliothek (University Library), the Freihaus ( Wiedner Hauptstraße), and Die Kopie (Printing Shop). The Resselpark (green campus park) is marked as an area overlay.

Map A - Static Map of TU Wien Campus

Legend

Kuppelsaal der TU Wien Kuppelsaal is the domed Hall of TU Wien. Used for formal events, academic gatherings, and representative university functions.
Bibliothek University library which is easily recognized by a large owl sculptures on its exterior and located directly across from Karlsplatz.
Freihaus Contains lecture halls, the Mensa, Mathematics, Cartography and Geoinformation departments and other buildings.
Die Kopie TU Wien Die Kopie is a practical student service point near campus, useful for printing, copying, and document preparation.
Resselpark Campus green space between the main buildings.

Map B - Interactive Leaflet Map of TU Wien

Extras & Excellence Points

  • Popup cards have eyebrow label, prominent place name, description text, and coordinate chips at the bottom that overrides Leaflet's default popup appearance
  • Resselpark drawn as a dashed green rectangle from bounding box coordinates, with a permanent "Resselpark" label sitting inside it
  • Map automatically fits its bounds to include all markers on load using fitBounds()
