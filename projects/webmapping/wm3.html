The Blue and Green Urban Space of Donaupark Wien

This basemap focuses on Donaupark and its surroundings. It maps green spaces, water bodies, roads, and buildings, and was designed in Maputnik to support the green and blue character of the area.

Interactive Vector Tile Basemap of Donaupark

This page uses MapLibre GL JS to display a custom basemap style saved as an external JSON file, with Vienna's open tree cadastre added as a live GeoJSON layer.

About Donaupark

Donaupark is a public green space in Vienna's 22nd district, Donaustadt. It includes open lawns, planted areas, walking paths, and the Irissee lake, and it is located close to the Alte Donau and the United Nations campus.

Design Choices

I created the basemap to support the green and blue space theme surrounding Donaupark. The colours become stronger as the user zooms in to make local detail appear more visible. Parks and forests shift from light green to richer green to emphasize vegetation, while buildings shift from pale beige to warmer beige so they remain useful for both orientation and visually secondary to the green and blue spaces. Zoom styling was also applied to widths for the same reason.

Excellence Highlights

  • The map includes a legend for some of the layers (not all) that helps users understand the design and the theme.
  • I added 7 extra styled layers and 9 additional filters.
  • A live GeoJSON layer from Vienna's open tree cadastre is added on top of the vector-tile basemap.
  • Tree features are clickable. Selecting a tree opens a popup with available species information from the data.
  • An additional marker of Alte Donau with a popup is added.
  • The basemap has a scale that helps orientation and also help users estimate real-world distances between Donaupark, the Alte Donau, paths, buildings, and tree locations
